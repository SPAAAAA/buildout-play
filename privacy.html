BuildOut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

What we store

  • A game account ID, created by Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication) when you first play.
  • Your game progress: display name, trophies and rank, B-Bucks, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, redeemed codes and match results.

Google login

If you choose "Log in with Google", Google confirms who you are and Unity Authentication links your Google account ID to your BuildOut account, so you can play on other devices. BuildOut only asks Google for basic sign-in. We do not store or use your email address, name or profile picture, and we never see your Google password.

Who processes the data

Accounts and game data are stored by Unity Gaming Services. Google login is provided by Google. The game files are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can play as a guest without logging in. To ask for your account and data to be deleted, open an issue at github.com/SPAAAAA/buildout-play/issues with your in-game player name (do not post personal details there).
